Origins and Vision

Tyneceploh Educational Foundation (TEF) was born from a lifelong dream envisioned by the late Mrs. Margret Ploh Chattah... The journey of TEF commenced with a groundbreaking program on January 25, 2015... TEF stands today as a testament of resilience, love, and education.
