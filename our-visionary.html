Origins and Vision

Tyneceploh Educational Foundation (TEF) was born from a lifelong dream envisioned by the late Mrs. Margret Ploh Chattah, a visionary with a deep passion for child development and education. Her dream began to take shape in the absence of her physical presence, but her spirit and determination laid the foundation for what would become a beacon of learning and opportunity.

The Journey Begins

The journey of TEF commenced with a groundbreaking program on January 25, 2015. Mrs. Chattah's vision was not just about constructing a building but about creating a safe, orderly, caring, and supportive environment where children could thrive academically, socially, and spiritually. Construction of the school building commenced in April 2015, marking the tangible manifestation of Mrs. Chattah's dream.

Challenges and Resilience

However, amidst the progress, challenges arose as Mrs. Chattah faced health issues that necessitated her treatment abroad. Despite her absence, her widower, Mr. Napoleon T. Chattah, along with dedicated supporters and well-wishers, persevered to keep the dream alive. TEF's resilience and commitment to Mrs. Chattah's vision became evident when the school officially opened its doors on September 3, 2018.

Fulfilling the Dream

Today, Tyneceploh Educational Foundation stands as a testament to Mrs. Chattah's unwavering dedication and belief in the power of education to transform lives. The school not only provides quality Christian education but also instills values of respect, responsibility, peace, and achievement in every student.
