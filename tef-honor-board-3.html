Honor Board standards

Recognition follows the rules used by the older TEF Honor Board.

Every recorded score must be 70% or higher.
90%+ average → Proprietor List.
85%+ average → Principal List.
80%+ average → Honor Roll.
Any recorded score below 70% makes the student ineligible.
Only students who qualify are displayed on the Honor Board.
