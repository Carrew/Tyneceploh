Our History

Tyneceploh Educational Foundation is a lifelong dream realized in the absence of the dreamer, the late Margret Ploh Chattah. Her passion for child development began a groundbreaking program on January 25, 2015. Construction started in April 2015.

She later fell ill and was taken for treatment abroad. The dream continued through her family and supporters and fully opened on September 3, 2018. Today, TEF stands as a living testimony of her vision and sacrifice.
